What is configuration-management-tool?

A deploy needs the same handful of moves every time: pick a group of hosts, run a sequence of commands against them, upload a few files, become somebody else first if you have to. cmt is that, on its own, without dragging in a full configuration-management system to do a job that doesn't need one — a from-scratch, pure-Go reimplementation of the idea behind pressly/sup, with an HCL2 manifest and connections handled by go-remoteexec/transport (local, SSH, WinRM, sudo/su/doas).
